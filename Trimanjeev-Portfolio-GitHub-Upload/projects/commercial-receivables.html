Commercial Performance & Receivables

Connect sales and margin analysis with an invoice-level collection queue for a simulated commerce business.

Independent project · Simulated data
COMMERCIAL PERFORMANCE
+21.7%Revenue growth

Northline Commerce · 2025 vs. 2024 · Simulated data

THE APPROACH

How the analysis works.

  1. 01

    Used SQL to normalize and validate invoice, sales-line and payment records, quarantining invalid and duplicate inputs.

  2. 02

    Built channel, product and customer reporting tables and a gross-profit bridge separating volume/mix, realized price and unit-cost effects.

  3. 03

    Calculated a fixed year-end receivables snapshot with payment-cutoff controls, aging buckets and ranked collection priorities; supplied a Power BI project.

KEY FINDINGS

What the numbers show.

FY2025 revenue rose 21.7% to CAD1.40 million while gross margin fell 1.70 percentage points to 42.0%; Marketplace margin fell 7.16 points.

CAD68,979 of receivables was overdue at year-end, and the three largest overdue accounts represented 57.7% of that balance.
