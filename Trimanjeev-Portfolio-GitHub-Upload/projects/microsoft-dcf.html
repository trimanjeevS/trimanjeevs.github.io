Microsoft Equity Research & DCF Valuation

Assess how growth, margins and capital investment shape Microsoft’s valuation in a dated historical research case.

Independent research · Historical valuation
MICROSOFT / EQUITY RESEARCH
$312.74Base-case implied value per share · USD
Historical valuation · 31 July 2025

THE APPROACH

How the analysis works.

  1. 01

    Linked FY2025 reported financials to a five-year operating forecast and unlevered free cash flow model.

  2. 02

    Modelled capital expenditure, finance-lease additions, working capital and terminal reinvestment before bridging enterprise value to equity value.

  3. 03

    Compared three operating cases and a 25-cell discount-rate and terminal-growth sensitivity grid.

KEY FINDINGS

What the numbers show.

The base case implied USD312.74 per share, 41.4% below the historical USD533.50 reference price on 31 July 2025.

Terminal value represented 81.6% of enterprise value, making long-term growth, reinvestment and discount assumptions central to the result.
